# Tinkerplex Labs

**Applied AI • RAG • Agentic Systems • Local AI • Privacy-Preserving Intelligence**

Tinkerplex Labs is an independent software and AI development organization
exploring practical applications of modern artificial intelligence.

Our interests include:

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agentic and multi-agent AI systems
- Local and privacy-preserving LLM deployment
- AI evaluation and observability
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Applied machine learning
- Human-centered AI tools

## Building, Learning & Sharing

We believe some of the most useful knowledge in AI comes from actually
building systems, comparing approaches, and sharing what worked — and
what didn't.

Alongside our development work, we participate in and organize technical
discussions, workshops, and community events for developers and
AI-enabled professionals.
