# Tinkerplex Labs

### Applied AI • RAG • Agentic Systems • Local AI • Privacy-Preserving Intelligence

**Tinkerplex Labs Inc.** is a Canadian software and artificial intelligence development company exploring practical ways to turn rapidly evolving AI technologies into useful, reliable systems.

We build, experiment, learn, and share what we discover along the way.

---

## What we're interested in

Our current areas of exploration include:

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — retrieval architectures, vector search, knowledge systems, and grounding
- **Agentic AI** — autonomous workflows, tool use, orchestration, and multi-agent systems
- **Local AI** — running capable language models on local hardware and reducing dependence on cloud infrastructure
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — connecting models safely and effectively to tools, data, and external systems
- **AI evaluation and observability** — understanding what AI systems actually did, why they did it, and whether the results can be trusted
- **Privacy-preserving AI** — architectures for applications where sensitive information should remain under the user's control
- **Applied machine learning** — using ML and generative AI to solve practical problems rather than simply demonstrate the technology

---

## Build. Learn. Share.

AI is moving too quickly for any one developer to have all the answers.

We believe some of the most valuable knowledge comes from **building real systems, comparing approaches, examining failures, and sharing what we learn with other developers**.

Tinkerplex Labs supports technical discussions and community events where developers, AI practitioners, and AI-enabled professionals can exchange ideas without requiring everyone to be an expert.

Topics range from hands-on RAG and agent development to broader questions about how AI is changing software development and professional work.
